## Overview

**Tecnología Urbana** is a startup focused on digital solutions for urban government operations — traffic management, incident tracking, and public services. The landing page functions as the company's corporate presence and entry point for government clients evaluating the product suite.

The company's main products are **Fiscalizador Digital** (mobile violation management for officers) and **SAI** (Sistema de Administración de Incidencias — a back-office platform for governments to process, manage, and follow up on violations from multiple sources).

---

## Role

**Frontend Developer** — responsible for building and delivering the corporate landing page.

---

## The Problem

Tecnología Urbana needed a single site that positioned the company — not just a specific product — to government decision-makers. The challenge is that "govtech startup" needs to look established and trustworthy before a government agency will take a meeting.

The landing had to present the company's mission, the product ecosystem, and a clear contact path — without overwhelming visitors with product details that belong on individual product pages.

---

## Technical Decisions

**Why Next.js for a corporate landing?**

Government agencies frequently access external sites from institutional networks with proxy configurations that can affect SPA load behavior. Server-side rendering with Next.js ensures the page delivers fully-rendered HTML on first request, avoiding blank screen states that would undermine the credibility objective.

**Brand consistency across a product suite:**

The site needed to visually connect to Fiscalizador Digital's landing page while maintaining a distinct corporate identity. Building both with the same stack (Next.js + TailwindCSS) and a shared design token system in Tailwind config made that possible without code duplication.

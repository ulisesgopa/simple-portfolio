## Overview

**Negocios Uno** is a platform that helps small and medium businesses manage their day-to-day operations. The client needed a professional landing page to generate leads and communicate the product's value to business owners who aren't necessarily tech-savvy.

This was my first paid freelance web project.

---

## Role

**Freelance Frontend Developer** — requirements gathering, design implementation, development, and delivery.

---

## The Problem

The client had a working product but no web presence. Potential users were landing on nothing after seeing the product mentioned on social media. The brief was clear: build a fast, professional site that converts visitors into contacts, with a two-week deadline.

---

## Technical Decisions

**Why Astro?**

The site is entirely marketing content — no login, no dynamic data, no interactivity beyond a contact CTA. Astro's static output means the page loads as raw HTML — no JavaScript parse time, no hydration overhead. For a landing page where every second of load time affects conversion, this was the right choice.

**Constraint-driven stack selection:**

Two weeks, solo developer, client with non-technical feedback cycles. Astro's file-based routing and component model made the project easy to reason about, easy to iterate on, and easy to hand off.

## What This Project Taught Me

Working with a real client for the first time taught me things no tutorial covers:

- **Requirements change mid-build** — building with components made pivoting painless
- **Non-technical feedback is specific but described visually** — "make it feel more serious" means adjusting font weight, spacing, and color contrast, not the layout
- **Delivery means deployed and working** — not just running locally

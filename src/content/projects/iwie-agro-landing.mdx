## Overview

**IWIE Agro** is a platform that helps farmers in Latin America manage agricultural processes digitally — crop cycles, inputs, harvests, and field records. The product sits in an interesting space: technically sophisticated but aimed at users with limited digital experience.

The landing page had to make that gap disappear. It needed to look modern and credible for investors while being immediately understandable for its actual end users.

---

## Role

**Freelance Frontend Developer** — design implementation, component architecture, and deployment.

---

## The Problem

Agro-tech platforms face a specific communication challenge: the people who approve the budget (investors, cooperatives) and the people who use the product daily (farmers, field technicians) have different expectations from the same site.

Too technical and you lose the farmers. Too simple and you lose investor credibility.

---

## Technical Decisions

**Why React + Vite over Astro?**

At the project's start I was already working in a React environment and the client expected future feature additions — a video demo, an interactive calculator. A SPA setup with Vite gave flexibility for those additions without needing to migrate the framework later. The tradeoff versus Astro's static output was acceptable given the roadmap.

**Mobile-first from the real target user:**

The primary end user is a farmer in a rural area, likely on a mid-range Android device on a 4G or 3G connection. That's not a theoretical constraint — it determined image compression choices, layout simplicity, and font size minimums.

---

## What This Project Taught Me

This was my first project where I had to design content strategy alongside the UI. "What should this section say?" was as important as "how should this section look?" — and answering it required understanding the end user, not just the client.

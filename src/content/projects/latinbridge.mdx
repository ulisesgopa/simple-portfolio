## Overview

**LatinBridge** is a B2B international trade platform connecting Latin American exporters with buyers in China. It operates across three locales with full multilingual routing, an AI-powered lead qualification assistant, and a protected admin dashboard where the sales team manages and tracks inbound leads through a full pipeline.

---

## Role
**Solo Developer**: multilingual landing, lead capture, admin authentication system, deal pipeline dashboard, SEO structured data, and AI assistant integration.

---

## The Problem

B2B export platforms operate across languages and cultures. A landing page that speaks only one locale loses qualified leads from the others. At the same time, without a lead management system, the sales team works out of email inboxes, with no tracking, no conversion metrics, and no pipeline visibility.

### Tech Stack

| Layer | Technology |

## Key Features

### Multilingual Routing (ES / EN / PT)

next-intl provides locale-aware URL structures (`/es`, `/en`, `/pt`) with automatic locale detection and validation. Each locale has its own message dictionary. The routing middleware redirects unlocalized URLs to the user's preferred locale based on the `Accept-Language` header.

### Lead Capture, Two Entry Points

The landing offers two conversion paths:
1. **Lead configurator modal**: a multi-step form where prospects define product category, volume, and target market
2. **AI micro-assistant**: a conversational interface that qualifies leads through guided questions before routing to the configurator

Both flows write to the same leads table in Supabase with a source tag, so the admin dashboard can compare conversion rates between entry points.

### Admin Dashboard with Lead Pipeline

The admin area is protected by a dual-layer authentication system:
- **Supabase JWT tokens** stored in HTTP-only cookies for session persistence
- **Email allowlist** check on every protected route, even with a valid JWT, only approved email addresses can access the admin area

The dashboard shows leads across four pipeline stages (`pending_quote → contacted → quoted → closed_won`) with conversion rate KPIs per stage, time-in-stage tracking, and status update controls.

### SEO, Structured Data

Each locale generates its own JSON-LD graph with Organization, WebSite, and Service schemas. Dynamic OG images are generated per locale for social sharing.

## Final Thoughts

International products have international problems. i18n is more than translation — it's locale detection in middleware, URL structure decisions, session cookie persistence across route prefixes, and content that actually resonates with each market. LatinBridge made those invisible complexities visible and concrete.

The AI qualification assistant also represents a different way of thinking about lead capture: some prospects won't fill a multi-step form on first contact, but they will have a conversation. Meeting users where they are — whether that's a structured form or a chat — is a product decision that affects conversion directly. Both paths land in the same database, which is how you measure which one works better.
